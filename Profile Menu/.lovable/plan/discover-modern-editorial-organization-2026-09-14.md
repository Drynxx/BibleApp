# Discover — Modern Editorial Organization

## Chosen direction
Apply the selected **Modern list hierarchy** while preserving the app’s existing identity:
- Keep the current warm ivory, terracotta, sage, and muted-gold palette.
- Keep Newsreader for editorial headings and Bible references, with DM Sans for controls and supporting text.
- Keep the current Home / Discover / Progress navigation unchanged.
- Keep visual artwork on plans rather than turning the whole page into plain text lists.

## Page hierarchy
- Tighten the opening area so **Discover**, its supporting sentence, and search read as one clear header zone.
- Keep search full-width with a quieter surface, lighter shadow, and stronger alignment.
- Refine the Plans / Books / Verses rail with equal-width labels, a precise terracotta underline, and clearer spacing from both search and content.
- Give the content area a subtle surface shift so the library feels distinct from the header without introducing another floating card.

## Plans organization
- Preserve one image-led featured plan, but reduce its height slightly so supporting choices appear sooner.
- Give the image overlay a cleaner information order: topic and verse count, plan title, then one short description.
- Present remaining plans as modern editorial rows with a small square artwork crop, clear reference information, and consistent chevrons.
- Keep the need filters compact and secondary to the section title.
- Improve section spacing and divider rhythm so “What do you need today?”, “Featured plan”, and “More plans” scan as distinct levels.

## Books organization
- Keep recently viewed books in a compact quick-access row.
- Refine Old and New Testament lists into calm, evenly spaced rows with stronger alphabetic scanning.
- Reduce unnecessary borders while retaining clear group headings and book affordances.

## Verses organization
- Use lightly surfaced list rows inspired by the selected direction, but avoid a stack of heavy floating cards.
- Make each Bible reference the strongest element, followed by translation/topic and a two-line excerpt.
- Align queue actions consistently at the right edge and make their purpose clearer.
- Strengthen separation between “For today” and “Popular” with spacing and restrained section markers.

## Queue and navigation
- Restyle the collapsed queue as a compact mini-player above the existing navigation, using the current terracotta action color.
- Keep expand/collapse and Begin now behavior.
- Ensure it has a defined edge and modest elevation but does not visually merge with verse rows or cover their content.

## Interaction and accessibility
- Preserve all existing tabs, topic filtering, universal search, previews, direct queue actions, and Inscribe link.
- Use quiet underline, opacity, and surface transitions only.
- Maintain keyboard focus, screen-reader labels, sufficient touch targets, contrast, and reduced-motion behavior.

## Validation
- Verify all three tabs, filters, search, preview sheets, queue actions, and navigation at 573×891 and the wider preview.
- Confirm featured plan artwork remains legible, references scan clearly, the queue does not obscure content, and no overflow or console errors occur.
