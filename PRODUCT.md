# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Stack

delegated: React Native with Expo (TypeScript). Chosen to provide a direct path to Google Play Store submission (AAB/APK generation), excellent offline SQLite data handling for full-text Scripture storage, native gesture and haptic feedback for memorization drills, and seamless cross-platform preview during local development.

## Users

Believers, students of Scripture, and devotional readers looking to commit Bible verses to memory, build daily devotional habits, and engage in disciplined Scripture meditation on Android mobile devices throughout their day (commutes, morning/evening quiet times, micro-breaks).

## Product Purpose

A focused Scripture memory and devotional app designed to transform passive Bible reading into active, lifelong retention. Success means users consistently practice, retain, and reflect upon verse collections through scientifically grounded recall drills without distraction.

## Positioning

Unlike general Bible reader apps (which emphasize passive scrolling and long-form commentary reading) or generic flashcard apps (which lack native Scripture context and book/chapter/verse hierarchies), BibleApp combines offline Scripture reading with progressive active recall mechanics (word-fade, fill-in-the-blank, first-letter recitation) and spaced repetition schedules tailored specifically to Scripture memorization.

## Operating Context

- Mobile handheld usage in short sessions (1-5 minutes per drill, daily review streaks) as well as dedicated quiet times (10-20 minutes).
- Frequently used in low-connectivity or offline environments (flights, subways, retreats, quiet spaces).
- Daily notifications and reminders for scheduled spaced-repetition reviews.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Offline-First Scripture Storage**: Preloaded public-domain translations (World English Bible - WEB, King James Version - KJV, Bible in Basic English - BBE) stored locally via embedded SQLite / local storage.
- **Active Recall Drills**: Progressive memorization levels including full-text view, gradual word-fade, first-letter prompt recitation, fill-in-the-blank, and self-graded flashcard review.
- **Spaced Repetition System (SRS)**: Smart review scheduling (e.g. SuperMemo SM-2 or Leitner-based intervals) prioritizing verses due for reinforcement.
- **Curated & Custom Collections**: Topical verse packs (peace, anxiety, faith, discipleship) plus ability for users to create custom collections and tag verses.
- **Progress & Streak Tracking**: Visual tracking of memorization mastery levels, daily streaks, and memory retention heatmaps.
- **Google Play Compliance**: Meets Android app performance, target SDK level, privacy guidelines, and app bundle packaging requirements.

## Brand Commitments

- Reverent, clean, and distraction-free interface.
- Zero intrusive ads or manipulative paywalls disrupting quiet contemplation.
- Warm, legible typography crafted for deep reading and active recall.

## Evidence on Hand

- Public-domain Bible text repositories and JSON/SQLite datasets (WEB, KJV, ASV).
- Established active recall and spaced repetition methodologies (SM-2, Leitner).

## Product Principles

1. **Active Over Passive**: True retention requires retrieval practice; reading is just step one, active recall is where memory forms.
2. **Offline by Default**: Scripture and personal memory progress must be 100% accessible anytime, anywhere, without reliance on a server or internet connection.
3. **Calm and Focused**: Every screen should foster stillness and reverence; no visual clutter, loud gamification, or distracting friction.
4. **Frictionless Daily Habit**: Make jumping into a quick 60-second review effortless from the home screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Dynamic font scaling and high-contrast readability settings for diverse eyesight needs.
- Full screen reader (TalkBack) support for verse navigation and memory prompts.
- Haptic feedback cues for interactive recall confirmations.
